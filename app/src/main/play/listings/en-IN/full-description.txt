Status PRO is the ultimate, lightweight companion designed to help you view, download, and organize temporary status updates from your favorite instant messaging platforms.

With a beautifully crafted, clutter-free user interface strictly following Material Design 3 guidelines and an elegant deep purple theme, our app makes managing transient social media cache and media updates a breeze.

✨ Key Features:
Instant Media Saving: Download seen status photos, stories, and videos in original, uncompressed high definition.
Auto-Categorization: Smart separation of images and video updates into clean, organized, and easily searchable tabs.
Built-in HD Media Viewer: Browse status photos in full screen with multi-touch zoom and play videos directly within the app using our smooth, responsive media engine.
Offline-First Architecture: Once loaded, view all your saved media seamlessly even without an active internet connection.
One-Tap Quick Actions: Repost, share, or set media as your device wallpaper with a single tap.
Flexible Share Hub: Seamlessly share cached media across any major messaging platform or social network (such as Instagram, Facebook, X, and more).
Personal & Business Support: Built-in dual modes to browse personal chat directories and business directories separately.
Rich Demo Mode: Instantly explore the app’s premium styling and full capabilities with pre-loaded high-quality sample content, even before connecting a folder.
🛠️ How to Use:
Watch the Statuses: Open your favorite instant messaging application and view the statuses you wish to save.
Open Status PRO: Launch this app to automatically sync and categorize the temporary media cache.
Save & Share: Tap the Download icon on any photo or video card to permanently back it up to your device's gallery, or share it directly to other social apps.
Android 11+ Setup: For devices running Android 11 and above, simply link the appropriate media cache directory once via our secure, standard Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) guide to automatically load all future updates!
🔒 Privacy & Permissions:
Privacy First: We respect your privacy. This application is completely offline-first and never uploads your files or personal data to external servers. All operations happen securely on your device.
Storage Access: The app utilizes Android's official Storage Access Framework (SAF) to request read-only directory access to transient status directories.
